# OT Intelligence Center — competitive integration increment

## Design patterns (original SGMA implementation)
Competitive research was based on public product positioning, not hands-on testing or a claim of best-in-class performance. No proprietary code, paid standard text, vendor assets or UI copied.

| Benchmark | Pattern adopted | SGMA extension |
|---|---|---|
| SecurityGate / OT Comply | Context-aware assessments and framework discovery | Explainable recommendations linked to explicit source and contract context |
| exida / VISCURITY | Engineering risk and design requirements | Lifecycle-specific acceptance methods; zone/conduit and asset modeling remain future work |
| CSF Tools | Searchable standards reference | Technology tags, original objective, evidence, candidate mapping and source revision/hash |
| SpecsIntact | Specification and acceptance deliverables | Native contract draft, traceability matrix and acceptance checklist tied to selected controls |
| Industrial Defender and other OT platforms | Evidence-driven assessment | Future vendor-neutral imports, not native live probing |

## Implemented
Local /ot page linked from /roadmap; 9 sectors, 7 system classes, 4 customer contexts, 7 lifecycle phases, 5 optional features. Search and independent selection of all 20 existing SGMA-derived controls. Deterministic suggestions explain why each control was included; sector/customer source references do not determine obligations. Original engineering acceptance candidates, source registry with reference-only/licensed/derived labels, source hash and revision. Export engineering pack JSON, native contract draft JSON and existing template selection JSON. Contract draft accepted by existing composition API; manual review, unassigned owner and no authority. The pack retains original candidate source IDs and hash; generated contractual drafts retain their provenance in rationale. The two exports represent alternative composition paths unless separately justified.
