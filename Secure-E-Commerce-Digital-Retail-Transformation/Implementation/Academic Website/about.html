About Us

Built for fans. Connected across Chico.

One-Punch Anime Emporium is a fictional specialty retailer serving the Chico, California area through four store locations and a shared online storefront.

The Business

More than a single storefront.

The company carries manga, figures, collectibles, apparel, accessories, and comics for customers across the Chico area.

Each location maintains its own inventory, while the online catalog gives customers a single place to search products and check availability before visiting a store.

The Customer Experience

Find it before making the trip.

Customers can browse the catalog, compare product availability across all four locations, add available merchandise to a cart, and complete a demonstration checkout workflow.

If an item is unavailable, the Special Orders workflow provides a separate path for requesting hard-to-find merchandise.

What We Carry

Six core product categories.

Manga

Popular series, new-reader starting points, and shelf-ready volumes.

Figures

Character figures and display pieces from major anime franchises.

Collectibles

Pins, replicas, display sets, and other fan-focused merchandise.

Apparel

Shirts, hoodies, hats, and other wearable merchandise.

Accessories

Bags, wallets, keychains, lanyards, and everyday fan gear.

Comics

Selected graphic novels and comic collections alongside the anime-focused catalog.

Project Note

Academic website. Portfolio-level implementation.

One-Punch Anime Emporium is a fictional organization created for an academic e-commerce project. The website has been expanded beyond the minimum assignment scope to demonstrate requirements analysis, multi-location inventory modeling, customer workflows, validation, checkout logic, and front-end implementation.
