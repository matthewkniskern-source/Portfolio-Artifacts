How It Works

  1. Submit the request. Tell us what you are trying to find.
  2. Staff reviews it. The request is checked for enough information to begin sourcing.
  3. Supplier availability is checked. Pricing and lead time are determined where possible.
  4. You approve the quote. The request does not become an order until the customer accepts the proposed price and terms.
